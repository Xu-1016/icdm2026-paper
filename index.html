Abstract

Tabular models can use schema information, such as column names and data types, to interpret cell values, but a uniform schema-semantic strategy may not benefit all samples equally. With a limited intervention budget, sample-column interventions require both within-sample action selection and cross-row budget allocation.

We construct matched terminal credit by comparing the default Static path with a single-column Complement path for the same sample. PairedMagnitude retains signed continuous gain, while PairedSign retains beneficial direction. Our Sign-to-Magnitude policy uses sign-based scores to select a candidate within each sample and magnitude-based scores to allocate the intervention budget across samples.

Research at a glance

Matched incremental supervision

Estimate the local value of a candidate intervention using paired terminal outcomes under controlled conditions.

Two-stage budget allocation

Select one candidate per eligible sample, then choose which samples receive interventions under the budget.
